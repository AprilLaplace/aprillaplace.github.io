The Lost Estate

沉浸式戏剧 Immersive Theatre
London, UK · 2024.11 — 2025.01

项目概述 Overview

伦敦 The Lost Estate 沉浸式戏剧制作，负责现场音频技术支持。沉浸式戏剧对声音的要求与传统镜框式舞台截然不同——观众分散在演出空间中，声音需要为每个位置创造同等的沉浸感。Immersive theatre production in London. Unlike traditional proscenium theatre, the audience is dispersed throughout the performance space, requiring multi-zone sound management for consistent immersion.

工作内容 What I Did

现场混音与音效控制，根据演出需求实时调整声音效果。与英国本土制作团队在纯英语环境中协作，熟悉多线叙事的舞台调度与执行。Live mixing and sound control with real-time adjustments. Collaborated with UK production teams in a fully English-speaking environment.
