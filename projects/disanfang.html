话剧《第三方指控》

"The Third Party Accusation" — Suspense Theatre
民国悬疑话剧 Suspense · 2025.09 — 至今 Present · 常州/上海

项目概述 Overview

民国悬疑话剧《第三方指控》，已巡演常州站与上海站。创排期间负责全流程声音设计，在悬疑戏剧语境下，声音作为叙事工具推动剧情、制造悬念。Republican-era suspense drama, performed in Changzhou and Shanghai. Full sound design created during the rehearsal period, where sound functions as a narrative device driving the plot and building tension.

工作内容 What I Did

创排期间负责声音设计，包括悬疑氛围所需的环境音效、关键情节音效及部分BGM的设计与制作。演出期间负责音响技术支持与控台。Designed environmental soundscapes, key plot sound effects, and BGM. Provided FOH mixing and technical support during performances.
