King's College London 龙年春晚

King's College London Chinese New Year Gala — Year of the Dragon
London, UK · 2024.01

项目概述 Overview

伦敦国王学院华人龙年春晚大型表演活动，节目涵盖现场乐队演出、小品、相声、器乐演奏、歌曲演唱、舞蹈表演等多种形式。负责现场混音及音响控制，确保各类型节目的声音呈现清晰、层次分明。A large-scale Chinese New Year gala at King's College London featuring live bands, comedy sketches, instrumental performances, vocal solos, and dance — with full live sound reinforcement and mixing.

工作内容 What I Did

负责与英方场地技术团队进行沟通协调，确保音响系统搭建符合演出需求。根据不同节目类型分别调试音频设备与混音参数——乐队需要舞台返听平衡，语言类节目侧重人声清晰度，歌舞类则兼顾音乐动态与唱词可懂度。Communicated with UK venue technical staff to coordinate sound system setup. Tailored mixing and equipment settings per act — balancing stage monitors for the band, optimizing vocal clarity for spoken word, and managing dynamic range for musical performances.

在演出全程实时调整混音参数，保障现场扩声系统稳定运行。在排练与演出现场协助流程管理与技术执行，确保各节目之间的顺利衔接。Managed real-time FOH mixing throughout the show, ensuring stable PA operation. Assisted with stage management and technical execution during rehearsals and the live event.
