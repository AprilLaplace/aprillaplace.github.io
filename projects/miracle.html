声音设计 / 现场PA Sound Design & PA

音乐剧《MIRACLE》

Musical Theatre — Peckham Fringe Festival
London, UK · 2024.01 — 2024.03

项目概述 Overview

英国 Peckham Fringe 戏剧节参赛作品《MIRACLE》，负责音乐剧的声音设计与现场混音。音乐剧要求同时处理演唱、对白和音乐三者的平衡，每次演出的动态变化都需要实时响应。Entry for Peckham Fringe Festival. Musical theatre demands simultaneous balancing of vocals, dialogue, and music, with real-time response to dynamic changes in every performance.

工作内容 What I Did

声音设计与现场混音，熟悉剧目 Cue 表及舞台流程。根据每个演员的唱段特点调整 EQ 和压缩参数，确保歌词清晰的同时保留音乐表现力。Sound design and live mixing. Adjusted EQ and compression per performer and musical number, maintaining lyrical clarity without sacrificing musical expression.
