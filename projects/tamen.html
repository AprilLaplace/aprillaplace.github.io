项目概述 Overview

央华戏剧出品舞台剧《她们》，担任音效设计与编辑，负责全剧声音设计的全流程工作。Full sound design for Magnificent Culture's stage production "Ta Men", covering the complete sound design workflow from concept to execution.

工作内容 What I Did

负责全剧声音设计，包括场景音效素材的制作与编辑、BGM选曲及精细编辑、以及整体声音风格的把握。确保声音设计与剧情节奏、舞台调度紧密契合。Designed and produced all sound effects, selected and edited BGM, and shaped the overall sonic aesthetic to align with the narrative rhythm and stage direction.

技术实现 Technical Approach

使用 QLab 进行多轨音频触发与实时控制，保障复杂音效编排的精准执行。完成 Cue 表编制，做到精确到秒级的时间线编排，并随排练进度持续迭代。Used QLab for multi-track audio playback and real-time control, with cue sheets timed to the second and iteratively refined throughout rehearsals.
